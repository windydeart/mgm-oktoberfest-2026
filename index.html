UNLEASHED EDITION

What's New in Oktoberfest 2026?

This year, mgm Vietnam is taking the celebration to a whole new level across Danang & HCMC offices!

Premium Craft Beer

Curated authentic German-style craft beers poured with cold fresh foam.

Bavarian Feast

Schweinhaxe crispy pork knuckle, grilled Bratwurst sausages, warm pretzels & specialty mustard.

Live Music & High Energy

Vibrant musical atmosphere, energetic tunes and dances for mgmies from 5:00 PM till late.

Interactive Mini-Games & Fun Activities

Exciting beer holding challenges, interactive mini-games, and celebratory photo booth moments.

DRESS CODE GUIDE

Attire Inspiration

"There's no official dress code. Casual is perfectly fine, but if you'd like to get into the Oktoberfest spirit, you're welcome to wear traditional Bavarian attire."

There is no mandatory dress code! Feel free to wear comfortable casual outfits. However, if you want to immerse yourself in the Oktoberfest spirit, traditional Bavarian attire is highly welcomed!

Lederhosen (Traditional Menswear) Dirndl (Traditional Womenswear) Casual / mgm Outfit (Comfortable & Free)
Bavarian Traditional Outfit Inspiration
